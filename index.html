Get in Touch

Every Samuel Snider garment begins in our Brooklyn studio where our small team works on every detail. The studio is a collaborative space where input is valued and decisions are made together. We value originality in process, refining ideas, folding in feedback, and evolving thoughtfully. Each garment is patterned, cut, sewn and refined in-studio, then produced with care in New York’s Garment District. This proximity allows for hands-on attention at every stage and helps maintain the integrity of our work. It also allows us to work at a pace that prioritizes quality over scale. Our knitwear, which requires different expertise, is crafted internationally by artisans who share our values
