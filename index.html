Tanu

A space for Indian art to travel.

Tanu Art House began as a small gallery giving emerging and established Indian artists a way to reach collectors beyond their city. Today the collection spans contemporary painting, sculpture, folk traditions like Madhubani and Warli, and limited-edition prints — each piece sourced directly from the artist's studio and shipped with full provenance.
